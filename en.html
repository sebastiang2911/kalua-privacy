Kalua Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy describes how the Kalua mobile application (the “App”), available for Android and iOS, collects, uses, stores and shares information about its users. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We only collect the information needed for you to create an account, build bowls and place orders:

  • Account data: username, email address and password. The password is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and is never stored in plain text.
  • Order data: the items you choose, quantities, prices, totals, date and status of each order, linked to your account to show your order history.
  • Inventory data (authorized staff only): if your account has a staff role, the inventory counts you record per location, together with your username and the date.
  • Minimal technical data: when the App talks to our servers, they may automatically log data such as your IP address and the request time, for security and operational purposes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers or payment data inside the App. The App does not include third-party analytics or advertising tools.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Session: access tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android).
  • Cart: your cart contents are stored locally on the device and contain no credentials.

This information is removed when you log out, clear the App's data or uninstall it.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and log you in.
  • Process your orders and show your order history.
  • Manage location inventory (authorized staff only).
  • Protect the App and our servers against misuse or fraud.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your data. We only share it with:

  • Infrastructure providers (server and database hosting) that process data on our behalf solely to operate the App.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights or our users' safety.

5. Security

All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS. Session credentials are kept in the operating system's secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and order data while your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except information we must keep to meet legal or accounting obligations.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. Email sebastiang2911@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Kalua account”.
  2. Send it from the email address registered to your account (or include it in the message).
  3. We will confirm the deletion within 30 days.

Deleted data: username, email, password, order history and inventory records linked to your user.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, update or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw your consent, by writing to the contact email.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with its update date. If changes are significant, we will notify you inside the App.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at sebastiang2911@gmail.com.
